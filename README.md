# Spatial Extension of the Geometric Matrix
### Rational Foundations for Modular 3D Systems

**Author:** Carolina Johnson (CJ)
**Date:** April 2026
**DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.19447062
**ORCID:** 0009-0002-8819-3347

## Overview

This paper extends the planar Geometric Matrix into three-dimensional Euclidean space, without introducing alternative volume definitions or floating-point drift. A discrete grid parameter `k`, constructed directly from the planar invariants, embeds the planar envelope into a deterministic 3D spatial construction. The result is a compact state vector `P = (b, c, k)`, from which the full planar geometry, spatial depth, and volume can be reconstructed.

## Core Result

For the Equilibrium Right state `[1, 5]`, the construction produces an exact integer volume:

**V = 4,800**

The Obtuse and Acute states evaluated in the paper produce irrational grid and volume values; the paper shows this reflects the specific states chosen rather than a general property of the non-equilibrium families.

Full derivations, worked examples, and the space-diagonal construction are in the paper.
